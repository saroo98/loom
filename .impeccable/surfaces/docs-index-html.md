# Public story: living route atlas

## Thesis

Loom is a living map from an owner request to earned execution. The website should make the route
visible without resembling an AI dashboard, generic SaaS landing page, or decorative technology
demo.

## Visual world

- Signal yellow is the terrain and the dominant brand field.
- Ink routes, survey marks, contour lines, and sealed paper notes show state and evidence.
- Cobalt is reserved for active selection and earned emphasis.
- Paper sections create calm between intense map fields.
- Corners are square, shadows are physical offsets, and no gradient or glass effect is used.

## Story and interaction

The opening states the contract, then immediately exposes an interactive route map. Three request
shapes demonstrate proportional planning: a small fix, a consequential system change, and an
unknown domain. Switching a route must update the request, current-world finding, domain state,
plan depth, proof medium, learning consequence, tier, and sealed outcome without hiding the
complete non-JavaScript story.

The remaining sequence explains the lifecycle, current-world trust, domain correctness, sealed
planning, real-medium proof, bounded owner learning, release evidence, useful refusal, and signed
installation.

## Accessibility and performance

- The route selector is a real keyboard-operable tablist with synchronized panel labeling.
- All essential content is semantic HTML and remains available without JavaScript.
- Focus states are visible, reduced motion disables nonessential movement, and increased contrast
  strengthens borders.
- System fonts and local SVG/CSS/JavaScript keep the site self-contained and offline-capable.
- Mobile converts the map into a readable vertical route without horizontal page overflow.

## Copy constraints

Every claim must be grounded in Loom 1.8.19 source, release evidence, or an explicit limitation.
Use short declarative sentences. Avoid inflated quality claims, generic AI language, comparison
copy, and fabricated outcomes.
